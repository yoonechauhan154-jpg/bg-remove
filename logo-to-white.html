Why Logos Need Clean Backgrounds

A logo stamped on a grey rectangle looks unprofessional on a white invoice or a colored shirt mockup. Sellers and designers keep a transparent master file and drop the logo onto whatever surface they need.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make a logo background transparent for free?

Upload the logo to BgClear and export as PNG with transparent background. The original background is removed automatically in your browser.

Can BgClear handle complex logo backgrounds?

Solid and lightly textured backgrounds work well. Busy photographic backgrounds behind a logo may leave artifacts — try exporting white first, then erasing remaining marks.

Will a white background behind a white logo work?

No — a white logo needs a dark or transparent background. Export transparent PNG and place it on the surface you need.
