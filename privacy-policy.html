Privacy Policy for BgClear

This Privacy Policy explains how BgClear handles information when you use our free AI background remover at https://bg-remover-free.netlify.app/.

What Data We Collect

BgClear is designed to respect your privacy. Our background remover processes images directly in your browser. We do not upload, save, store, sell, or share the images you select for background removal.
