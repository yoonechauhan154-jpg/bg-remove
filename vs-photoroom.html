Prepare a product cutout without a BgClear account

  1. Use a sharp photo with visible contrast between the product and its background.
  2. Select the photo in BgClear and allow the model to load and process it locally.
  3. Review the edges, handles, gaps and any product details that must remain visible.
  4. Choose White for a white-background export, or Transparent and PNG for reuse in another editor.

JPG cannot preserve transparency. Check the marketplace's current image requirements separately; changing the background alone does not guarantee that a listing image meets them.

Check quality before processing a catalog

Use several representative photos: an opaque product, a white item on a pale background, fine detail and a difficult case such as glass. Compare downloaded outputs on a dark and light background at the same displayed size.

Record missing parts, halos, export dimensions and any manual cleanup required. BgClear can struggle with translucent objects, logos, stickers and text. Do not assume a larger model fixes every difficult image, and do not infer that either tool is more accurate without testing.

For a timing comparison, record first-use model loading separately from repeat processing. On an older phone, start with one photo before attempting a batch.
