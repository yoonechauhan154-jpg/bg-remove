About BgClear

BgClear is a free, browser-based background remover I built because I got tired of the same old cycle: tools that make you create an account, tools that watermark your work until you pay, and tools that send your photos to a server somewhere before returning a "premium" result. This one does none of those things.

1

Our Purpose

Most people don't need a $20-a-month subscription to remove a background. They need to clean up a product photo for a marketplace listing, fix a profile picture before a job interview, or strip the background off a scanned document before submitting homework. Those are small, everyday jobs, and they shouldn't require learning Photoshop or paying for software you'll use twice.

So the purpose here is deliberately narrow: one task, done well, with nothing in the way. No layers, no brushes, no ten-minute tutorial before your first upload. You open the page, drop in a photo, and the background is gone.

AI

How the Tool Works

Under the hood, BgClear runs an AI model that identifies the main subject of an image — the person, product, animal, or object — and separates it from everything around it. The part people usually find surprising is where that happens: right in your browser.

Your photo is processed locally, on your own device. It is never uploaded to a server, never stored, never looked at by anyone on our side. That's what makes the tool both fast and private. It's also why there's no account system — there's nothing to save, so there's nothing to log in for.

Why We Made This Tool

The honest answer: the free options that existed when we started were not really free. Some capped you at a handful of images per week. Some plastered a watermark on every download and then upsold you on removing it. Others wanted an account, an email address, and a credit card just to test a feature that takes five seconds to run.

That friction punishes exactly the people who need the tool most — students, small sellers, freelancers, and anyone doing a one-off job. We made BgClear to remove those barriers: no signup, no watermark, no image limits on the free tier, and no requirement to hand over a photo you'd rather keep to yourself.

Questions People Actually Ask Us

Is BgClear really free, or is there a catch?

Really free. No trial, no credit system, no weekly cap on images. The site is ad-supported, which is how it pays for itself without charging users. That's the whole business model.

Do my photos get stored anywhere?

No. The AI model runs in your browser and your images are processed on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, so there is nothing to store — and nothing to leak.

Why is there no signup?

Because there's nothing to save on our end. No accounts means no passwords to manage, no emails to hand over, and no reason to lock features behind registration.

What if the AI gets the cutout wrong?

Try the photo once more with better light or a plainer background — input quality is the biggest factor. Some tricky cases (fine hair, see-through objects, low-res files) are genuinely hard for every tool, including paid ones.

Can I use the downloaded images commercially?

Yes. Images you process with BgClear are yours to use for work, listings, or anything else. We add no license strings and claim no rights to your files.
