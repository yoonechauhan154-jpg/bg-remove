BgClear vs Clipdrop — Free Background Remover Comparison

🔒 Your photo never leaves your device

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Full disclosure before we start: I run BgClear, so this comparison is written with a bias I'd rather be honest about. I've also used this tool in real work, and I'll point out where it genuinely beats us. No tool is the best at everything — this page is about which one fits your specific job.

1. Pricing: Clipdrop vs BgClear

Clipdrop (from Stability AI) offers background removal alongside a broader creative suite — uncrop, cleanup, relight, and more. It runs on a credit system: free users get a small daily allowance, and anything serious needs a paid plan with monthly credits.

BgClear has no credits and no daily cap. You can remove backgrounds all day for free, ad-supported. What you don't get is Clipdrop's wider toolkit, which is genuinely useful for creators who do more than cutouts.

2. Signup & Friction: Clipdrop vs BgClear

Clipdrop encourages an account so it can track credits across sessions. BgClear is anonymous by design — there is no login because there is no account state.

3. Privacy & Where Images Are Processed

Clipdrop processes images server-side under Stability AI's terms. BgClear runs entirely in the browser; your image never leaves your device, which matters for sensitive or unreleased work.

4. Image Quality & Hard Edges

Clipdrop's models are capable, and on tricky edges it benefits from server-scale compute. BgClear holds up well on standard subjects; on fine hair, glass, or fur, we'd expect Clipdrop to edge ahead, and we'd rather admit that than argue it.

6. Which Should You Choose?

  • Pick BgClear if you want no signup, no watermarks, no credit limits, and images that never leave your browser.
  • Pick Clipdrop if its paid features — templates, resizing, bulk tools, or an app — are worth the subscription or credit cost to you.
  • Try both on the same photo. The best remover is the one whose result you'd actually put on your website, and you can't read that from a spec sheet.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Clipdrop?

Clipdrop is a suite of AI image tools from Stability AI that includes background removal, uncrop, relight, and cleanup, priced on a monthly credit model.

Is Clipdrop free?

Clipdrop gives free users a small daily credit allowance; the background remover works within it, but regular use runs out quickly and requires a paid plan.

Which is better for occasional users?

For a handful of cutouts a month, both work. BgClear has no limits to track, so there's nothing to budget. Clipdrop is more interesting if you already use its other tools.
