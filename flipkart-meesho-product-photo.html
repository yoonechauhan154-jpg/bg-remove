Flipkart & Meesho Product Photo Background (Seller Guide)

🔒 Your photo never leaves your device

Selling on Flipkart or Meesho? Your product photos need to meet strict background and dimension rules before they go live. This guide covers every requirement both platforms enforce, compares them side by side, and walks you through a bulk workflow that processes 20+ catalog images without sending a single file to a server. If you have ever had a listing rejected for "image quality" or "background mismatch," this page will explain exactly why it happened and how to fix it for good.

Flipkart Product Photo Requirements

Flipkart is one of India's largest online marketplaces with over 350 million registered users, and its image guidelines exist to keep the catalog looking uniform across millions of listings. A product photo that does not follow these rules will either be rejected during upload or quietly demoted in search results, so getting the background right is not optional — it directly affects your visibility and sales.

The main product image (the one that appears in search results and product grids) must use a pure white background. Flipkart specifies that the background color should be RGB 255, 255, 255, which means the entire canvas behind the product is completely white with no gradients, no shadows, and no textures. This is not the same as "light gray" or "off-white" — the platform's automated checks compare the background pixels against the exact RGB value, and anything that deviates will trigger a rejection.

Your product must fill at least 85% of the image frame. This is another area where sellers commonly fail. If your product occupies only half the canvas and the rest is empty white space, Flipkart considers it a poor-quality listing. The rule exists so that products look large and detailed in grid view where thumbnails are quite small. Aim to crop tightly around the product while leaving a small, even margin on all sides — typically 5% to 8% of the canvas width works well.

The minimum resolution for Flipkart catalog images is 500 x 500 pixels, but the recommended resolution is 1,000 x 1,000 pixels. Higher resolution images look sharper in zoom view and on high-DPI mobile screens, which is where the vast majority of Flipkart browsing happens. Flipkart accepts JPEG and PNG formats. For catalog listing images, the file size must stay under 50 KB, which means you will almost certainly need to compress your JPEGs. For detail page images (the larger images visible on the product detail page), file sizes up to 5 MB are allowed.

Flipkart strictly prohibits watermarks, logos, promotional text, or any kind of overlay on the main product image. Your product photo should show only the product itself on a white background. No "SALE" badges, no store name watermarks, no price tags — nothing that is not part of the physical product. This rule is enforced during upload review, and violating it is one of the most common reasons sellers contact support asking why their listing was rejected.

Flipkart requires multiple images for each listing. While one main image is the minimum, you should upload between three and five images showing the product from different angles — front, back, and side views at a minimum. For apparel, this typically means front view, back view, a close-up of fabric texture, and a model or flat-lay shot. For electronics, you might include the front, the ports and buttons on the side, and the packaging. The additional images can use lifestyle or in-context backgrounds, but the main image must remain pure white.

Meesho Product Photo Requirements

Meesho has grown rapidly to become one of India's most popular social commerce platforms, particularly strong in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities. Because many Meesho sellers are small businesses and resellers who may not have professional photography equipment, the platform enforces strict image rules to keep the catalog consistent and trustworthy for buyers who are often making their first online purchase.

The main product image on Meesho must have a white background, and the platform is noticeably stricter about this requirement than many other Indian marketplaces. Meesho's automated image review system checks the background color of every uploaded photo, and even slightly off-white backgrounds — caused by uneven lighting during photography, slight color casts, or compression artifacts — can trigger a rejection. The background needs to be uniformly white across the entire image, not just in parts of it.

The minimum resolution for Meesho product images is 600 x 600 pixels, which is slightly higher than Flipkart's minimum. The recommended resolution is 1,000 x 1,000 pixels. Meesho primarily accepts JPEG format for product images. The product should be centered within the frame and clearly visible against the white background, with enough contrast that the product's edges are sharp and easy to distinguish.

Like Flipkart, Meesho does not allow watermarks, promotional text, or logos on product images. The image should contain only the product itself. Meesho also recommends uploading between three and five images per listing, covering multiple angles and providing enough visual information for the buyer to make a purchasing decision without seeing the product in person.

One important thing to understand about Meesho is that the platform uses a reseller model. This means your product photos may be shared and reshared by thousands of resellers across WhatsApp, Facebook, and other social platforms. Clean, professional product photos on a white background get shared more often because they look credible and polished. Poorly lit photos with cluttered backgrounds not only risk rejection by Meesho's review system but also reduce the likelihood that resellers will choose your product to promote.

Bulk Workflow for Indian Sellers

If you are selling on both Flipkart and Meesho (which many Indian sellers do), you likely have dozens or even hundreds of product photos that need background removal and resizing. Doing this one image at a time is not practical. Here is a bulk workflow that processes 20+ product images using BgClear's batch mode, with no signup, no watermarks, and no files leaving your device.

  1. Photograph your products. Use a plain background (it does not have to be perfect white — any light, solid-colored surface works) and consistent lighting. Natural light from a window is usually sufficient. Place the product on a table near the window, shoot from slightly above, and avoid using the flash. Aim for well-lit, evenly lit photos with no harsh shadows. If you are shooting apparel, hang or lay the item flat. If you are shooting hard goods, place them on a rotating stand or simply photograph each side manually.
  2. Batch upload to BgClear. Open BgClear in your browser and use the batch upload feature. You can process up to 20 images at a time. Select all the product photos you want to process — BgClear will remove the original background from each one and generate transparent PNG files. Everything runs locally in your browser, so no images are uploaded to any server. If you have more than 20 images, simply run the batch process again with the next set.
  3. Download transparent PNGs. After processing, download all the transparent PNG files. These are your clean product cutouts with no background at all. At this stage, the images are on a transparent canvas, ready for you to place on any background you need.
  4. Add white background in bulk. To add a pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255), you can use a simple batch tool. On Windows, the built-in Photos app can do this one at a time — open the transparent PNG, select "Edit," choose a white canvas, and save. For true bulk processing, free tools like GIMP with a batch script, IrfanView with batch conversion, or ImageMagick from the command line can place every transparent PNG onto a white canvas in one operation. On Mac, Preview combined with Automator can handle this in bulk.
  5. Resize to platform specifications. Both Flipkart and Meesho prefer 1,000 x 1,000 pixel images. Use the same batch tool to resize every image to 1,000 x 1,000 px. For Flipkart catalog images specifically, compress the JPEG to under 50 KB. Most batch conversion tools let you set a target file size or quality level — a JPEG quality setting of 70 to 80 usually lands you under 50 KB for a 1,000 x 1,000 product photo. Meesho images do not have the same strict file size limit, so you can use a higher quality setting (85 to 90) for those.
  6. Upload to Flipkart and Meesho. Log in to each platform's seller dashboard, navigate to your catalog or add new products, and upload the prepared images. The main image (first image in the sequence) should be the white-background cutout. Additional images can be the original lifestyle or in-context photos if you kept them.

This workflow eliminates the need for expensive photo editing software or professional editors. A seller with 50 products and three images per product (150 images total) can complete the entire process in under two hours, from photography to upload, using only BgClear and a free image editor. The key advantage is that your product photos never leave your device during the background removal step, which matters if you are working with unreleased products or sensitive catalog information.

Common Rejection Reasons on Flipkart & Meesho

Understanding why product photos get rejected saves you time and prevents your listings from sitting in review limbo while potential sales are lost. Here are the most common reasons both platforms reject product images, along with how to fix each one.

Non-white background. This is the number one rejection reason across both platforms. Even if your background looks white to your eyes, slight color casts from warm lighting, reflections from nearby surfaces, or compression artifacts can push the background away from pure RGB 255, 255, 255. Using BgClear to remove the original background entirely and then placing the product on a digitally created white canvas is the most reliable way to guarantee compliance. When you create the white background digitally, you know the exact RGB value because you chose it yourself — there is no ambiguity.

Blurry or low-resolution images. Both Flipkart and Meesho reject photos that appear blurry, pixelated, or too low-resolution. On a modern smartphone, this is usually not an issue if you hold the camera steady and tap to focus on the product before shooting. Avoid using digital zoom, which degrades image quality. If your phone's camera produces images larger than 1,000 x 1,000 pixels, that is fine — you can always resize down, but you cannot resize up without losing quality.

Watermarks or text overlays. Any text, logo, watermark, or promotional overlay on the main product image will cause an automatic rejection. This includes semi-transparent watermarks that you might think are subtle enough to pass automated review. Both platforms use image analysis tools that detect overlaid text patterns, so even small, faint watermarks in the corner of the image will be flagged.

Wrong dimensions or aspect ratio. Product photos that are not square (i.e., not a 1:1 aspect ratio) or that do not meet the minimum resolution requirements get rejected. Flipkart requires at least 500 x 500 pixels and Meesho requires at least 600 x 600 pixels. Rectangular images, panoramic crops, or images with black bars on the sides to force a square format are all rejected. Always shoot or crop to a perfect square.

Product too small in frame. Flipkart specifically requires that the product fills 85% or more of the image area. If there is too much empty white space around the product, the listing may be flagged. Meesho's requirement is less precisely defined but follows the same principle — the product should be the dominant element in the frame, centered and large enough to show detail. Cropping your photos tighter around the product is the simplest fix.

Poor lighting or heavy shadows. While not an explicit rule in either platform's guidelines, photos with harsh shadows, dark corners, or uneven lighting often get flagged during manual review. Natural, diffuse lighting produces the best results. If you are shooting indoors, position your product near a large window and use a white poster board on the opposite side as a simple reflector to fill in shadows.

Frequently Asked Questions

What background color does Flipkart require for product photos?

Flipkart requires a pure white background with RGB values of 255, 255, 255 for the main product image. The product must fill at least 85% of the frame, and there should be no watermarks, logos, or text overlaid on the image. Secondary images may use lifestyle or in-context backgrounds, but the primary catalog photo must be on white.

What size should Meesho product images be?

Meesho requires a minimum resolution of 600x600 pixels for product images, with 1000x1000 pixels recommended for best display quality. The images should be in JPEG format, and the product should be centered and clearly visible against a mandatory white background.
