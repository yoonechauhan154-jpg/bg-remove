Frequently Asked Questions

Is this background remover free?

Yes, 100% free. No signup, no watermark, no hidden charges.

Can I remove backgrounds from multiple images at once?

Yes. Select or drag up to 20 images together and BgClear will process them one by one. You can download each result individually or all at once.

What image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, WebP up to 10MB.

Can I add a white background or change the format?

Yes. After processing, choose White, Black, or any custom color from the Background menu, and pick PNG, JPG, or WebP from the Format menu. Previews and downloads update instantly.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, works on all devices — mobile, tablet, desktop.

Is my image stored on servers?

No. All processing happens in your browser. Images are never uploaded.

How accurate is the AI background removal?

Very accurate for people, products, and objects with clear edges.

How is BgClear different from other "free" background removers?

Most "free" background removers upload your photo to their servers for processing — even the ones that don't charge you. BgClear is different: the AI model runs entirely in your browser using WebAssembly and ONNX runtime. Your photo never leaves your device, which means there's zero risk of it being stored, shared, or used for training.
